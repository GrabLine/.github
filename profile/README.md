# GrabLine

**Fast, free downloads on your PC and your Android phone.**

**[Website](https://grabline.github.io/) · [Download for desktop](https://github.com/GrabLine/GrabLine/releases/latest) · [Download for Android](https://github.com/GrabLine/grabline-android/releases/latest) · [Documentation](https://github.com/GrabLine/GrabLine/blob/main/docs/README.md)**

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GrabLine/GrabLine/main/docs/screenshots/downloads.png" width="820" alt="GrabLine on a PC with downloads in progress" />

</div>

GrabLine downloads files over parallel connections, picks video and audio quality,
handles torrents and server folders, and keeps it all in one list. The Android app
downloads on its own and pairs with the PC over a QR code, so a link you find on your
phone can download on the computer at home.

## Principles

- Free and open source under [AGPL-3.0-or-later](https://github.com/GrabLine/GrabLine/blob/main/LICENSE).
- No ads, no GrabLine account and no usage telemetry.
- Phones and PCs talk to each other over your own network. Nothing goes through a
  GrabLine server, because there is none.
- Downloads and the integrations you turn on contact their own services. The
  [privacy policy](https://github.com/GrabLine/GrabLine/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) has the details.

## Help and contribute

Start with the [documentation](https://github.com/GrabLine/GrabLine/blob/main/docs/README.md),
the [troubleshooting guide](https://github.com/GrabLine/GrabLine/blob/main/docs/troubleshooting.md)
or the [Phones and PCs guide](https://github.com/GrabLine/GrabLine/blob/main/docs/devices.md).
Ask questions in [Discussions](https://github.com/GrabLine/GrabLine/discussions/categories/q-a).
Report a bug in the repository it belongs to: [desktop and extension](https://github.com/GrabLine/GrabLine/issues)
or [Android](https://github.com/GrabLine/grabline-android/issues), with your version,
platform and exact steps.

Read [Contributing](https://github.com/GrabLine/GrabLine/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
before sending changes. Report a vulnerability privately through the affected
repository's Security tab; see the [security policy](https://github.com/GrabLine/GrabLine/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

If GrabLine helps you, you can [support development on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/zain021xd).
